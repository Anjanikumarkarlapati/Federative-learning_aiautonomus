## Reading (14 Oct 2026 run)

* The setting is too hard to resolve most options: base accuracy is ~23% after 20 rounds on 9 classes (chance 11%), and **rare-hazard accuracy is 0% in every arm and seed** - at a 6% rare share the model never predicts a hazard class in this budget, so this run cannot test whether focal loss or the KST score helps rare hazards.
* Only two differences have a 95% CI excluding zero, both costs: SCAFFOLD adds ~4.2 MB/round (the control-variate uplink), and `kst_ucb` selection delivers ~0.6 fewer vehicle updates per round (uptime is no longer a gate). SCAFFOLD's +2.0 points of last-5 accuracy is borderline.
* No option, and not all options together, measurably improves accuracy, worst-vehicle or worst-10% accuracy here. A longer run (more rounds, larger rare share) is needed before any of them is claimed.
